CONTINUE?

The games you started deserve an ending.

Every gamer has a pile of unfinished games. Every app built to manage that pile is a spreadsheet with box art — and a spreadsheet of eighty-seven unplayed games is a guilt machine. CONTINUE? treats the backlog the way an arcade treated a game over: not as failure, but as a second chance, a countdown, and a coin.

What it does

Save

Share a game from a video, a thread or a store page straight into your pile. A sheet slides up over the app you're already in and adds it in about two seconds — it even works with no signal.

Decide

Set how much time you have and what mood you're in, then pull the lever. The cabinet deals three games from your own pile and tells you why each one matched.

Finish

Clearing a game triggers a full credits roll of your own stats. Then rank it head to head against what you've already beaten — no stars, just your real top ten.

Never nags

No interstitials, no banners, no notifications guilt-tripping you. Ads exist, but only when you choose to watch one, and only in exchange for something.
