Privacy Policy

CONTINUE?

Effective 15 August 2026 · Last updated 12 September 2026 · Applies to the Android app com.mikhilnaika.continueapp

The short version. CONTINUE? has no accounts and no sign-in. Your game pile, your coins, your rankings and your settings live on your phone, not on a server. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos library or location.

Three things do involve other companies: Google AdMob serves the optional rewarded ads you choose to watch, RevenueCat and Google Play process purchases, and our own small server looks up game information for you. What each of them receives is spelled out below.

No account No name or email No location permission No photo library access Works offline

1. Who runs this app

CONTINUE? is an independent app built and operated by Mikhil Naika. For anything in this policy — questions, complaints, or a data deletion request — contact naikamikhil@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device and never leaves it

The following is stored locally on your phone. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, or visible to us in any form:

  • Your pile — the games you add, their status (Now Playing, Cleared, The Pile, Wanted, Retired), your stacks, notes and verdicts.
  • Your rankings, trophies, statistics and draw history.
  • Your coin balance and Free/Pro state cached on device.
  • Your settings, including the haptics and clipboard toggles.
  • Cover art and game images cached for offline use.
  • The offline game index that ships inside the app.

Images you share into the app are processed entirely on your device. When you share a screenshot to CONTINUE?, the text in it is read by Google ML Kit's on-device text recognizer. The image is never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and the app never requests access to your photo library.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

3. What the app sends to our own server

CONTINUE? talks to a small backend of ours at continue-worker.gamestoplay.workers.dev, hosted on Cloudflare Workers. It exists so that game data can be fetched without shipping API keys inside the app. It sends:

WhatWhenWhy
The search term you type When you search in DISCOVER To look the game up and return matches
Text you deliberately share into the app (for example a video caption) Only when you use the Android share sheet to send something to CONTINUE?, and only if the app cannot already identify the game offline To work out which game the text refers to
A game's numeric ID When a screen needs details or key art for a game To fetch that game's information

No identifier is attached to these requests. There is no user ID, no device ID, no advertising ID and no cookie in them, so we cannot tie one request to another or build any profile. We do not log the contents of your requests.

As with any internet request, our host (Cloudflare) sees the originating IP address. It is used only to enforce rate limits that stop the service being abused, and it is not stored by us or joined to anything. Search results are cached for up to 24 hours, keyed by the search term itself, so that repeated lookups are fast and cheap.

If you are offline, the app falls back to its on-device index and database and sends nothing at all.

3a. Sharing a game or a card with someone

CONTINUE? can share a game with a friend, and can make an image summarising your pile, a game you cleared, or your all-time top ten.

Nothing about a share reaches us. The image is drawn on your phone from data already on your phone, saved into the app's own private cache, and handed to whichever app you choose in Android's share sheet. The message and the link are assembled on your device too. Your phone does not contact our server in order to share anything, and we are never told that you shared, what you shared, or who you shared it with.

What you share is your choice, and it is visible to whoever receives it. A pile card shows your total hours and game count; a cleared card shows a game, its length and its position in your ranking; a high-scores card shows your top ten. Once you send it, it is in the hands of the app and the people you sent it to, exactly like any other photo or message. Nothing is posted anywhere on your behalf and nothing is shared automatically.

Links you send are public. A shared game link looks like continue-worker.gamestoplay.workers.dev/g/1942 — a game's numeric ID and nothing else. It contains no identifier for you, so it cannot be traced back to you or to your pile, and anyone holding the link sees the same page as anyone else. When someone opens it, their device (or their messaging app, generating a preview) requests that page from our server and loads the game's artwork from IGDB, as described in section 6. That request comes from their device, not yours.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. We never see or receive your payment details — Google does not share them with us.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and entitlements. RevenueCat issues your install an anonymous app user ID (a random string, not linked to your name, email or Google account) and records your purchase history against it, so the app knows whether Pro is active. We do not send RevenueCat your name, email address or any other personal identifier.

See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

5. Advertising

CONTINUE? shows rewarded ads only. There are no interstitials, no banners and no ads that appear on their own. An ad plays only after you tap a button that says what you get for watching it, and you can always decline.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. Per Google's published disclosure, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares the following for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID.
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general (city-level or broader) location of the device.
  • Interactions such as app launches, taps and ad views.
  • Diagnostic information about app performance.

This data goes to Google and its advertising partners, not to us. We receive only aggregate earnings reports, which contain no information about individuals.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, CONTINUE? shows you Google's consent form the first time you open the app, and requests no ads at all until you have answered it. You can change that answer whenever you like: open the YOU tab and tap AD PRIVACY CHOICES. If you decline, or withdraw consent later, no ad is ever requested — and nothing else changes. Your pile, the draw, ranking, sharing and offline use all work identically; you are simply not offered the watch-an-ad-for-a-coin exchange, and you can still earn coins by clearing games or buy them outright.

Your controls: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID, or to opt out of ad personalisation entirely. The app keeps working if you do. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Buying Pro removes ads from the app entirely.

6. Game information

Game titles, cover art, release dates, ratings and playtime estimates come from IGDB.com. The data was freely provided by IGDB.com.

Game data — titles, dates, genres, playtime estimates — is requested by our server, never by your device, so IGDB's data API never sees anything about you.

Cover art and key art are different. Those images are served from IGDB's image CDN at images.igdb.com, and your device loads them from there directly, the same way any app or web page loads an image hosted elsewhere. That means IGDB's CDN sees your device's IP address and the images you requested, as any image host would. We do this because proxying every cover through our own server would be slower, would cost far more, and would not meaningfully change what IGDB could infer. IGDB's own privacy policy governs what they do with it. No identifier of ours travels with those requests.

7. Children

CONTINUE? is not directed to children. It is rated for users aged 13 and over, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data through this app, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Deleting your data

  • Everything on your device: uninstall the app, or use Settings → Apps → CONTINUE? → Storage → Clear data. This is immediate and complete.
  • Purchase records held by RevenueCat: email naikamikhil@gmail.com from any address and ask for deletion. Installs are anonymous, so to locate the right record please include the Google Play order ID from your purchase receipt email (it looks like GPA.0000-0000-0000-00000). We will action the request within 30 days.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, as described above.
  • Purchase and refund records held by Google Play are Google's; manage them through your Google account.

We hold no server-side account for you, so there is nothing else of yours for us to delete.

9. Retention

Cached search results expire within 24 hours. We keep no request logs containing your queries. RevenueCat retains purchase history for as long as the app is in service or until you ask for deletion. Data on your device is kept until you delete it.

10. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS; the app explicitly disables cleartext networking. Our backend holds no personal data, so there is no user database to breach. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but the strongest protection here is structural: we do not collect the data in the first place.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live — for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA in California — you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to its processing. Because we hold no personal data about you beyond an anonymous purchase record, most such requests are satisfied by the deletion steps in section 8. Write to naikamikhil@gmail.com and we will help. We do not sell or share your personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA.
